# Voice

Playful, precise, generous.

- **Back every joke with a real number.** "src/auth is on fire: 212 commits in 30 days", not "src/auth is a dumpster fire".
- **Never mock a codebase.** Fire is activity, moss is stability, ruins are honest notes. Say so when it matters.
- **No hype words:** revolutionary, leverage, AI-powered, blazing fast, game-changing.
- **Short sentences, active voice, concrete nouns.** Districts, towers, roofs, commits.
- **Claims only after testing.** Status labels say exactly what was tested ("pasted on Paper 1.21.11").
- **Minecraft is a destination, not our name.** Say "works with Minecraft Java Edition"; keep the disclaimer on every public surface.

Examples

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "Visualize your tech debt with AI!" | "TODOs crumble. 73 of them in ReactFiberCommitWork.js." |
| "Blazing fast rendering" | "Linux, 96k files, renders in 25 s on a laptop." |
| "Your messy repo" | "Your busiest district" |
